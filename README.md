# Shopify Store Business Plan - Premium Health & Wellness

A comprehensive business plan for building a highly successful Shopify store in the health and wellness niche with the potential to generate **$100,000+ per month** in revenue.

## Overview

This repository contains a detailed business plan for launching and scaling a premium health and wellness e-commerce store on Shopify. The health and wellness industry is one of the fastest-growing markets globally, projected to reach $7 trillion by 2025.

## Why Health & Wellness?

- **High Demand**: Growing health consciousness among consumers
- **High Margins**: Premium products command 50-70% gross margins
- **Recurring Revenue**: Supplements and wellness products are consumable, driving repeat purchases
- **Subscription Potential**: Strong opportunity for monthly subscription boxes
- **Low Competition Barrier**: Quality and branding differentiate successful stores

## Key Highlights

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Monthly Revenue Goal | $100,000+ |
| Average Order Value | $75-$110 |
| Gross Margin | 50-65% |
| Net Profit Margin | 25-30% |
| Time to Goal | 12 months |
